System Architecture

How EduGuard AI Works

01

Data Collection & Ingestion

Student data (attendance, grades, socio-economic flags, behavioral notes) is ingested from school management systems via secure APIs. Data is anonymized and encrypted at rest.

02

ML Risk Scoring Pipeline

A gradient-boosted ensemble model (XGBoost + LSTM for temporal patterns) scores each student daily on a 0–100 risk scale, weighted by 14 predictive features.

03

Alert Generation & Routing

Students crossing thresholds (Caution ≥ 50, High ≥ 70, Critical ≥ 85) trigger automated alerts routed to the appropriate counsellor, teacher, or administrator.

04

Intervention Recommendation Engine

Based on root-cause analysis (financial, academic, social, attendance), the system recommends targeted interventions — scholarship applications, tutoring, counselling, or parental outreach.

05

Outcome Tracking & Model Retraining

Intervention outcomes are logged and feed back into the model. Continuous learning improves prediction accuracy over each academic term.

Every EduGuard module directly maps to objectives in the National Education Policy 2020, ensuring compliance, grant eligibility, and systemic impact.

EduGuard's mobile app and community hubs extend quality learning to children in remote, tribal, and migrant communities — directly addressing NEP's GER targets.

✔Special Education Zones (SEZ)

Flexible schooling management and targeted financial aid align with NEP's SEZ framework for aspirational districts with high dropout vulnerability.
